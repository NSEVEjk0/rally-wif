Watch a booking and a full refund.

Rally lets you book a place in a workshop, meetup or feedback session. You pay with WIF, a cryptocurrency token. The host sets how many bookings are needed. If there are too few by the deadline, you can claim your full payment back. Watch the narrated recording below.

Controlled-wallet technical demonstration. The host and participant wallets were controlled for this demo. No independent customers or actual workshop attendance are claimed.

What happened in this recording

  • First event: a participant booked a place for 0.01 WIF. There were too few bookings by the deadline, so the participant claimed the full payment back.
  • Second event: the host copied the first event with new dates, and a participant booked again. The participant cancelled before the deadline and got a full refund. The host cancelled the event and withdrew the deposit.
  • All demo payments and deposits were refunded or withdrawn. The contract’s balance and amounts owed were zero. Network fees for the demo totalled 0.00002846110128 ETH.

Official WIF on Robinhood Chain, chain 4663. Contract: 0x71C615c6Fa167a0843DaD6Ed715B8736b7f507DA.
